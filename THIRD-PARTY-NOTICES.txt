[A3UE] AI FPV Drones - third-party notices
==========================================

This mod contains modified copies of six functions from Antistasi Ultimate
(Antistasi Ultimate 12.0.3 and 12.4.1 carry identical license terms):

  A3A_fnc_singleAttack
  A3A_fnc_wavedAttack
  A3A_fnc_attackHQ
  A3A_fnc_invaderPunish
  A3A_fnc_showInterceptedSetupCall
  A3A_fnc_showInterceptedSupportCall

The compiled scripts also contain macros from Antistasi Ultimate's core
headers. That code is used under the MIT License. The copyright and
permission notices below are reproduced from Antistasi Ultimate's LICENSE
file, covering Antistasi Ultimate and the projects it descends from.

This mod contains no files from the FPV drone packs it supports (FPV Drone
Crocus, Ukraine FPV Drone, KVN fiber-optic FPV); it uses their drones only
when those mods are loaded.
